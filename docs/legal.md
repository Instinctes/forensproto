# Legal & acceptable use

ForensProto is published under the [MIT License](../LICENSE).

Use it only when you have a legal right to the data:

- your own wallets, backups, and devices
- an investigation you are authorized to run
- estate / inheritance cases with documented authority

Do **not** use it to access wallets, accounts, or machines you do not own or are not authorized to examine. Password recovery, seed reconstruction, and nonce analysis are dual-use. The authors do not help with unauthorized access and are not liable for how you run the software.

Secrets (seeds, private keys, recovered passwords) must stay on your machine. Do not paste them into GitHub issues.

If you are unsure whether your use is lawful, stop and ask a lawyer — not the issue tracker.
